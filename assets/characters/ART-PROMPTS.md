# Character artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Existing Kenshiro, Rei, Toki, Spade and Heart artwork is preserved. New assets cover 29 combat characters and 13 story characters. Combat sheets contain six poses: guard, two walking poses, windup, strike and hit reaction. The engine reuses these poses for each character's moves; these are not bespoke animations for every special attack.

`manifest.json` and `manifest.js` describe the prepared PNGs consumed by the game and gallery. `sources/` contains the generated originals. Preparation retains alpha, separates poses and aligns their feet. Run `python3 prepare_character_art.py NAME SOURCE.png HEIGHT`, or add `--portrait` for story cutouts. Dependencies: Pillow and NumPy.
